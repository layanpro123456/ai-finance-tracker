10 Integrated Modules
⚡

AI Wealth & 40+ Currencies

Log expenses in everyday speech: “Spent Rs 4,500 on dinner at Keells” or “Paid €35 for groceries”. Powered by Google Gemini Flash with automatic category detection and zero hardcoded symbols across USD, LKR, EUR, GBP, and INR.

📍

Geofenced Payment Sentinel

Register waypoints (100m–1500m radii). Arriving at a supermarket alerts you to your remaining produce budget; arriving at your landlord prompts 1-click rent settlement; meeting a friend reminds you to settle your dinner split.

🚗

Traffic-Resilient Commute

Haversine velocity tracking classifies walking, cycling, and driving. In stop-and-go city traffic (0–15 km/h) or red lights, the engine remembers peak speed history and locks into vehicle mode rather than misclassifying your drive as walking.

⛽

1-Click Commute Fuel Ledger

Auto-calculates daily transit distance and estimated fuel or ticket fares (with idle traffic factor). A single click records the travel cost directly into your financial ledger under Transportation and adjusts account balances.

📋

Financial Task Bridge

Connect operational to-dos directly to bank balances. Checking off payable bills (e.g. WiFi Bill) auto-deducts funds; completing receivable milestones deposits income. Features a gamified daily “No-Spend” streak challenge.

📦

Asset Vault & Net Worth

Catalog laptops, phones, vehicles, and collectibles. Tracks initial purchase price vs. current market valuation to compute real-time Net Worth, with automated warranty expiration countdown alerts.

🔄

Subscription Burn Rate Hub

Monitors monthly and annual recurring commitments with renewal countdowns, active bank account linking, and direct 1-click cancellation links to eliminate subscription creep.

🤝

Social IOU & Dinner Splits

Track peer debts seamlessly (“They Owe Me” vs “I Owe Them”). Settle balances in 1 tap, automatically recording the reimbursement transaction and updating contact ledgers.

🖥️

Desktop Route Simulator

Test velocities, traffic jams, and geofence arrivals without leaving your desk. Includes pre-built scenarios (Walk to Supermarket, Highway Express, City Traffic Jam) and a dynamic speed slider (0–100 km/h).
